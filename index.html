Got Questions?

Are the workshops hands-on?

Yes, all our workshops are interactive and completely hands-on. We recommend bringing your own laptop with the required development environment pre-installed. Setup instructions will be emailed two weeks prior to the event.

Is there a virtual attendance option?

Absolutely. DevConf 2026 offers a fully featured Virtual Pass that grants you live access to all keynote streams, interactive Q&A sessions, and a digital networking lounge to connect with global attendees.

Are meals and beverages included in the ticket?

Yes! An in-person standard ticket includes a catered breakfast, lunch, and all-day coffee/snack stations. We also offer vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options—just specify your dietary requirements during registration.

Will session recordings be available after the conference?

All keynote and breakout sessions will be recorded and made available to ticket holders within 48 hours of the conference closing. You'll have unlimited access to the VOD library for up to 6 months.
